A book is more than a product. It is a world an author has created, and it deserves a marketing approach that understands its value.

Helen Fisher specialises in book promotion and marketing, bringing together creative positioning, audience awareness and practical campaign planning to help authors strengthen their presence in a busy publishing landscape.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked questions.

Who do you work with?

Self-published and independent authors, as well as writers preparing a new release, across fiction and non-fiction genres.

Can you help with an existing book?

Yes. Backlist titles benefit from fresh positioning, a refreshed Amazon presence and renewed promotion just as much as new releases.

When should I get in touch?

Ideally a few months before launch, but it is never too late to improve how your book is presented and found.
